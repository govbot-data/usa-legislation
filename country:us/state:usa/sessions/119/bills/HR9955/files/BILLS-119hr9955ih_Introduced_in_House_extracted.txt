1.Short titleThis Act may be cited as the Pathway to Trades Act. 2.Agreements with institutions for Federal Perkins Loans Section 463(a) of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 1087cc(a)) is amended—

(3)by inserting after paragraph (8) the following:  (9)provide that the institution of higher education will—
