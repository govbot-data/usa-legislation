(B)LimitationNo Federal funds may be provided under this section to any non-Federal entity—(i)that is domiciled in, headquartered in, or organized under the laws of, or the principal place of business of which is located in, a foreign country of concern (as defined in section 10638 of the Research and Development, Competition, and Innovation Act (42 U.S.C. 19237)); or(ii)that has in place any agreement or partnership with a foreign country of concern..October 22, 2026Reported with an amendment, committed to the Committee of the Whole House on the State of the Union, and ordered to be printed
