119th CONGRESS2d SessionS. CON. RES. 37IN THE SENATE OF THE UNITED STATESCONCURRENT RESOLUTIONAuthorizing the use of the rotunda of the Capitol for a ceremony to honor the late Senator Lindsey O. Graham.1.Use of rotunda for a tribute honoring Lindsey O. GrahamThe rotunda of the Capitol is authorized to be used on July 28, 2026, for a tribute to honor the late Senator Lindsey O. Graham. Physical preparations for the event shall be carried out in accordance with such conditions as the Architect of the Capitol may prescribe.Passed the Senate July 21, 2026.Secretary
