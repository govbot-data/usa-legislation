Begun and held at the City of Washington on Saturday, the third day of January, two thousand and twenty six



Concurrent Resolution

  Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),
SECTION 1. USE OF ROTUNDA FOR A TRIBUTE HONORING LINDSEY O. GRAHAM.  The rotunda of the Capitol is authorized to be used on July 28, 2026, for a tribute to honor the late Senator Lindsey O. Graham. Physical preparations for the event shall be carried out in accordance with such conditions as the Architect of the Capitol may prescribe.
