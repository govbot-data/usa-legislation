To amend title 41, United States Code, to amend the time period for certain acquisition-related dollar thresholds.


1.Short titleThis Act may be cited as the Ensuring Federal Purchasing Efficiency Act. 2.Ensuring Federal purchasing efficiency through adjustment of certain acquisition-related dollar thresholds every 3 yearsSection 1908(c)(2) of title 41, United States Code, is amended by striking of each year evenly divisible by 5 and inserting , 2028, and every 3 years thereafter.
