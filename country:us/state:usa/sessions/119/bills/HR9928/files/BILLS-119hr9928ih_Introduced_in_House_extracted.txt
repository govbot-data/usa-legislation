R. 9928IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVESJuly 23, 2026Mr. Perry introduced the following bill; which was referred to the Committee on the JudiciaryA BILLTo prohibit the Secretary of Homeland Security from waiving or reducing any fee associated with an application for United States citizenship. 
1.Short title
 This Act may be cited as the No Discounts for Foreigners Act. 
2.Prohibition on certain fee waivers and reductions 
(a)ProhibitionExcept as provided for under subsection (b), the Secretary of Homeland Security may not waive or reduce any fee associated with an application for United States citizenship. (b)ExceptionThe prohibition under subsection (a) shall not apply to an application submitted pursuant to section 328 or section 329 of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1439; 1440) by a member of the Armed Forces or an alien who previously served as a member of the Armed Forces. 
(c)Armed Forces definedIn this section, the term Armed Forces shall have the meaning given such term in section 101 of title 10, United States Code.
