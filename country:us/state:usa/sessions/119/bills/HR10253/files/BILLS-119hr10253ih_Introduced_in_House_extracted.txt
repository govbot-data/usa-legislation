R. 10253IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVESSeptember 3, 2026Mr. Carter of Louisiana (for himself, Ms. Letlow, Mr. Fields, Mr. Higgins of Louisiana, Mr. Scalise, and Mr. Johnson of Louisiana) introduced the following bill; which was referred to the Committee on Oversight and Government ReformA BILLTo designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 1706 South Burnside Avenue in Gonzales, Louisiana, as the Cpl. Lawrence Brown Post Office Building. 
1.Cpl. Lawrence Brown Post Office Building 
(a)DesignationThe facility of the United States Postal service located at 1706 South Burnside Avenue in Gonzales, Louisiana, shall be designated as the Cpl. Lawrence Brown Post Office Building.  (b) References Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the Cpl. Lawrence Brown Post Office Building.
