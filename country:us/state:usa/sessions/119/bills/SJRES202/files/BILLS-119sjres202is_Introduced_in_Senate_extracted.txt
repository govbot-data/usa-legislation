IIA119th CONGRESS2d SessionS. J. RES. 202IN THE SENATE OF THE UNITED STATESJuly 23, 2026Mr. Markey introduced the following joint resolution; which was read twice and referred to the Committee on Small Business and EntrepreneurshipJOINT RESOLUTIONProviding for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5,
      United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Small Business Administration relating to
        Citizenship and Residency Requirements and Recission of Procedural Notice
        5000-872050 and Revised Applicant Ownership, Citizenship, and Residency
        Requirements for 7(a) and 504 Loans.
    
 That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Small Business Administration relating to Citizenship and Residency Requirements and Recission of Procedural Notice 5000-872050 (issued February 2, 2026) and Revised Applicant Ownership, Citizenship, and Residency Requirements for 7(a) and 504 Loans (issued February 11, 2026), determined to be a rule in a letter of opinion by the Government Accountability Office dated July 1, 2026, and printed in the Congressional Record on July 14, 2026, on pages S3929 through S3931, and such rule shall have no force or effect.
