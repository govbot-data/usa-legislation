II119th CONGRESS2d SessionS. 5498IN THE SENATE OF THE UNITED STATESSeptember 24, 2026Mr. Warner introduced the following bill; which was read twice and referred to the Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and PensionsA BILLTo amend the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act to add alpha-gal to the definition of major food allergen.1.Short titleThis Act may be cited as the Alpha-gal Allergen Inclusion Act.2.Adding alpha-gal to definition of major food allergen(a)In generalSection 201(qq) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (21 U.S.C. 321(qq)) is amended by adding at the end the following:(3)(A)Galactose-alpha-1,3-galactose (commonly known as alpha-gal).(B)For purposes of this subparagraph, the term galactose-alpha-1,3-galactose includes—(i)ingredients derived from non-catarrhine primate mammals; and(ii)red algae in the order Gigartinales.(C)For purposes of this subparagraph, the term galactose-alpha-1,3-galactose does not include mammal-derived ingredients for which the Secretary determines there is no galactose-alpha-1,3-galactose above an established, detectable limit (such as ingredients made from alpha-gal-knockout mammals)..(b)Effective dateThe amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply beginning on the date that is 18 months after the date of enactment of this Act.
