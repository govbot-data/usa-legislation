R. 9927IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVESJuly 23, 2026Mr. Panetta (for himself and Mr. Feenstra) introduced the following bill; which was referred to the Committee on AgricultureA BILLTo amend the Food Security Act of 1985 to add the emergency watershed program as a covered program for purposes of carrying out the regional conservation partnership program, and for other purposes. 
1.Short titleThis Act may be cited as the Farmers Lifeline to Overcoming Disasters (FLOOD) Act. 2.Emergency watershed programSection 1271A(1) of the Food Security Act of 1985 (16 U.S.C. 3871a(1)) is amended by adding at the end the following: 
 
(G)The emergency watershed protection measures described in section 403 of the Agricultural Credit Act of 1978 (16 U.S.C. 2203)..
