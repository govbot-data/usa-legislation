IV119th CONGRESS2d SessionH. RES. 1629IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVESOctober 19, 2026Mr. Fine (for himself and Mr. Weber of Texas) submitted the following resolution; which was referred to the Committee on Oversight and Government ReformRESOLUTIONExpressing support for the designation of the third Wednesday of October, as Support Your Local Chamber of Commerce Day. 
Whereas Chambers of Commerce are champions of communities, striving to address the community’s top challenges and greatest opportunities; Whereas, through bold leadership and innovative problem solving, Chambers of Commerce foster stronger, more vibrant communities; 
Whereas Chambers of Commerce advocate for priorities to promote economic prosperity, enhancing the quality of life of the community; Whereas Chambers of Commerce create access to meaningful employment and opportunity for all residents; 
Whereas Chambers of Commerce serve as a trusted resource for businesses, residents, and other stakeholders; Whereas Chambers of Commerce create catalytic community partnerships to build resilient economies and promote community-wide prosperity; and 
Whereas the third Wednesday of October, would be an appropriate date to designate as Support Your Local Chamber of Commerce Day: Now, therefore, be it That the House of Representatives supports the designation of Support Your Local Chamber of Commerce Day.
