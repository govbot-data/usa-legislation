R. 10279IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVESSeptember 3, 2026Mr. Mast introduced the following bill; which was referred to the Committee on Oversight and Government ReformA BILLTo designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 9995 Southeast Federal Highway in Hobe Sound, Florida, as the Special Operations Chief Petty Officer Aaron Carson Vaughn Post Office Building.1.Special Operations Chief Petty Officer Aaron Carson Vaughn Post Office Building(a)DesignationThe facility of the United States Postal Service located at 9995 Southeast Federal Highway in Hobe Sound, Florida, shall be known and designated as the Special Operations Chief Petty Officer Aaron Carson Vaughn Post Office Building.(b)ReferencesAny reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the Special Operations Chief Petty Officer Aaron Carson Vaughn Post Office Building.
