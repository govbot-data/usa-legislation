II119th CONGRESS2d SessionS. 5495IN THE SENATE OF THE UNITED STATESSeptember 24, 2026Mr. Kim (for himself and Mr. Heinrich) introduced the following bill; which was read twice and referred to the Committee on Agriculture, Nutrition, and ForestryA BILLTo amend the Richard B. Russell National School Lunch Act to exclude certain military housing allowances from the calculation of household income, and for other purposes.1.Short titleThis Act may be cited as the Military Dependents School Meal Eligibility Act of 2026.2.Exclusion of basic allowance for housing from income calculationSection 9(b)(13) of the Richard B. Russell National School Lunch Act (42 U.S.C. 1758(b)(13)) is amended by striking on behalf of and all that follows through provision of law,.3.Report on direct certification(a)In generalNot later than October 1, 2026, the Secretary of Agriculture, in consultation with the Secretary of Defense, shall submit to Congress a report on the feasibility and cost of establishing procedures under which children who are members of a household of a member of a uniformed service may be certified as eligible for free or reduced price lunches under the Richard B. Russell National School Lunch Act (42 U.S.C. 1751 et seq.) and free or reduced price breakfasts under section 4 of the Child Nutrition Act of 1966 (42 U.S.C. 1773) without further application.(b)ContentsThe report required under subsection (a) shall evaluate a range of options for implementing the direct certification described in that subsection, including—(1)the use and sharing of data collected for the calculation of the basic needs allowance under section 402b of title 37, United States Code, and other assistance programs for members of a uniformed service; and(2)the exclusion of non-service member income from the calculation of household income under the Richard B. Russell National School Lunch Act (42 U.S.C. 1751 et seq.).
