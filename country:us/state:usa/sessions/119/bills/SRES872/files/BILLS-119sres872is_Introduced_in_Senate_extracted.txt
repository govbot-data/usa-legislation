III119th CONGRESS2d SessionS. RES. 872IN THE SENATE OF THE UNITED STATESSeptember 22, 2026Mr. Booker (for himself and Mr. Tillis) submitted the following resolution; which was referred to the Committee on Foreign RelationsRESOLUTIONCelebrating the 65th anniversary of the establishment of the Peace Corps, and commending its volunteers and contributions to global peace, friendship, and development.Whereas on March 1, 1961, President John F. Kennedy issued his historic call to service and established the Peace Corps, launching a new era of American volunteerism abroad; Whereas over the past 65 years, nearly 250,000 Americans have served as Peace Corps volunteers in 144 countries, fostering people-to-people diplomacy in areas such as education, health, agriculture, environment, youth in development, and community economic development;
 Whereas the Peace Corps continues to exemplify the toughest job you'll ever love, building sustainable partnerships and strengthening communities around the world;Whereas the Peace Corps has enhanced global perceptions of the United States by demonstrating American values through service, humility, cultural exchange, and partnership rather than through political or military means;Whereas returned Peace Corps volunteers bring home invaluable linguistic, cultural, and technical skills that strengthen the United States workforce, expand America’s global competency, and enhance the Nation’s diplomatic and economic competitiveness;Whereas the Peace Corps has contributed to United States national security by strengthening international stability, promoting economic growth, and addressing root causes of conflict through education, empowerment, and community development;Whereas Peace Corps volunteers have exemplified American leadership through volunteerism, generosity, and service to others; andWhereas Peace Corps service has inspired generations of Americans to pursue careers in public service, education, economic development, diplomacy, and health, creating a lasting pipeline of globally minded civic leaders; Now, therefore, be itThat the Senate—(1)celebrates the 65th anniversary of the founding of the Peace Corps;(2)honors the nearly 250,000 individuals who have served as Peace Corps volunteers for their dedication to peace, friendship, and community-driven development; and(3)commends the Peace Corps’ enduring mission to put purpose, passion, and skills to work for the United States and the world.
