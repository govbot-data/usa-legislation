I119th CONGRESS2d SessionH. R. 10086IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVESAugust 13, 2026Ms. De La Cruz (for herself and Mr. Correa) introduced the following bill; which was referred to the Committee on Energy and CommerceA BILLTo reauthorize the National Diabetes Prevention Program. 
1.Short titleThis Act may be cited as the Diabetes Prevention Program Reauthorization Act of 2026. 2.Reauthorization of National Diabetes Prevention ProgramSection 399V–3(d) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 280g–14(d)) is amended by striking such sums as may be necessary for each of fiscal years 2010 through 2014 and inserting $39,300,000 for fiscal year 2027, $44,300,000 for fiscal year 2028, $49,300,000 for fiscal year 2029, $54,300,000 for fiscal year 2030, and $59,300,000 for fiscal year 2031.
