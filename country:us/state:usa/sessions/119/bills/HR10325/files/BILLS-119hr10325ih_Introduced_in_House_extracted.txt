R. 10325IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVESSeptember 8, 2026Ms. Waters introduced the following bill; which was referred to the Committee on Financial ServicesA BILLTo increase the mandatory contribution amount of Federal Home Loan Banks to the Affordable Housing Program, and for other purposes. 
1.Increased affordable housing program investmentSection 10(j)(5) of the Federal Home Loan Bank Act (12 U.S.C. 1430 (j)(5)) is amended— (1)in subparagraph (C), by striking In 1995, and subsequent years, and inserting In 1995 through 2025,; and
(2)by adding at the end the following:  (D)In 2026, and subsequent years, 15 percent of the preceding year's net income, or such prorated sums as may be required to assure that the aggregate contribution of the Banks shall not be less than $100,000,000 for each such year..
