R. 9851IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVESJuly 22, 2026Mr. Flood introduced the following bill; which was referred to the Committee on Ways and Means, and in addition to the Committee on Energy and Commerce, for a period to be subsequently determined by the Speaker, in each case for consideration of such provisions as fall within the jurisdiction of the committee concernedA BILLTo amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to require providers of services to provide patients access to clergy. 
1.Short titleThis Act may be cited as the Protecting Access to Spiritual Treatment and Organized Religion Act of 2026 or the PASTOR Act of 2026. 2.Requiring providers of services to provide patients access to clergySection 1866(a)(1) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1395cc(a)(1)) is amended— 
(1)in subparagraph (X), by striking and at the end; (2)in subparagraph (Y), by striking the period and inserting , and; and 
(3)by inserting after subparagraph (Y) the following new subparagraph:  (Z)beginning January 1, 2028, upon request of an individual receiving items and services from such provider and notwithstanding any waiver under section 1135, to allow a member of clergy designated by such individual access to such individual, provided that such clergy member adheres to such safety measures and other protocols as may be specified by the Secretary (in consultation with providers of services, States, and religious organizations)..
