II119th CONGRESS2d SessionS. 5690IN THE SENATE OF THE UNITED STATESOctober 15, 2026Mr. Warnock (for himself and Mr. Scott of South Carolina), under authority of the order of the Senate of September 30, 2026, introduced the following bill; which was ordered read twice and referred to the Committee on Energy and Natural ResourcesA BILLTo provide for the replacement of the plaque at Lincoln Memorial commemorating the I Have a Dream speech of Martin Luther King, Jr.1.Short titleThis Act may be cited as the Renewing the Dream Act.2.Replacement of plaque at Lincoln Memorial(a)In generalThe Secretary of the Interior shall remove the plaque at Lincoln Memorial placed pursuant to Public Law 106–365 and install a new plaque commemorating the speech of Martin Luther King, Jr., known as the I Have a Dream speech.(b)Relation to CWAChapter 89 of title 40, United States Code (commonly known as the Commemorative Works Act), shall apply to the design and placement of the new plaque.(c)Acceptance of contributionsThe Secretary of the Interior may accept contributions toward the cost of preparing and installing the new plaque without further appropriation.(d)Federal fundsFederal funds may be used to—(1)remove the plaque placed pursuant to Public Law 106–365; and(2)design, procure, and install the new plaque.
