        JOINT RESOLUTION
        Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of
            title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Environmental Protection
            Agency relating to California State Nonroad Engine Pollution Control Standards;
                Small Off-Road Engines Regulations; Notice of Decision.
    
    
        
 That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Environmental Protection Agency relating to California State Nonroad Engine Pollution Control Standards; Small Off-Road Engines Regulations; Notice of Decision (90 Fed. Reg. 640 (January 6, 2025)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
