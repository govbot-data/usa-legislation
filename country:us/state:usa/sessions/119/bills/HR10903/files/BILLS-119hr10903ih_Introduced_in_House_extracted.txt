R. 10903IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVESOctober 22, 2026Mr. Vasquez introduced the following bill; which was referred to the Committee on Veterans' AffairsA BILLTo direct the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to seek to enter into a lease for the purpose of establishing a Vet Center in Socorro, New Mexico, and for other purposes.1.Short titleThis Act may be cited as the Socorro Vet Center Act. 2.To direct Secretary of Veterans Affairs to seek to enter into lease for purpose of establishing Vet Center in Socorro, New MexicoThe Secretary of Veterans Affairs shall seek to enter into a lease—(1)commencing not later than December 31, 2027; and(2)for the purpose of establishing a Vet Center (as such term is defined in section 1712A(h) of title 38, United States Code) in Socorro, New Mexico.
