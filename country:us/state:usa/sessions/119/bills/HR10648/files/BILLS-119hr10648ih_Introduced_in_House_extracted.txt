R. 10648IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVESOctober 1, 2026Ms. Boebert introduced the following bill; which was referred to the Committee on Veterans' AffairsA BILLTo direct the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to furnish inpatient treatment under the Veterans Community Care Program for a mental health condition, and for other purposes. 
1.Short titleThis Act may be cited as the Veterans’ Mental Health Access Act. 2.Furnishing of inpatient treatment for a mental health condition under the Veterans Community Care Program (a)In generalThe Secretary of Veterans Affairs shall furnish covered care to a covered veteran. 
(b)TimelineThe Secretary shall furnish covered care under subsection (a) not later than 24 hours after the covered veteran requests covered care or a health care provider prescribes covered care to the covered veteran. (c)ReportEvery six months, the Secretary shall publish on a website of the Department of Veterans Affairs a report regarding the wait time for a veteran seeking covered care furnished— 
(1)in a medical center of the Department of Veterans Affairs; or (2)under the Veterans Community Care Program. 
(d)DefinitionsIn this section: (1)The term covered care means inpatient treatment for a mental health condition, including post-traumatic stress disorder, depression, or substance abuse, furnished— 
(A)in a medical center of the Department of Veterans Affairs; or (B)under the Veterans Community Care Program. 
(2)The term covered facility means, with respect to a covered veteran, the medical center or hospital of the Department of Veterans Affairs— (A)that is closest to the residence of the covered veteran; and 
(B)where the Secretary furnishes covered care sought by the covered veteran. (3)The term covered veteran means a veteran described in section 1703(b) of title 38, United States Code. 
(4)The term Veterans Community Care Program means the program established by section 1703(a) of title 38, United States Code.
