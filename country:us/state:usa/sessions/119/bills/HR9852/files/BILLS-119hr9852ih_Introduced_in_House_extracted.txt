R. 9852IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVESJuly 22, 2026Mrs. Foushee introduced the following bill; which was referred to the Committee on Transportation and InfrastructureA BILLTo amend title 23, United States Code, to include super speeder programs in State highway safety programs, and for other purposes. 
1.Short titleThis Act may be cited as the Slow Down Act. 2.Highway safety programsSection 402(a)(2)(A) of title 23, United States Code, is amended— 
(1)by redesignating clauses (xiii) and (xiv) as clauses (xiv) and (xv), respectively; and (2)by inserting after clause (xii) the following: 
 
(xiii)to establish and implement Stop Super Speeders programs requiring the installation and use of active intelligent speed assistance technology, that automatically limits a motor vehicle from exceeding the applicable speed limit, for individuals with records of repeated or severe violations of applicable speed limits including penalties for failure to comply with such requirements in their motor vehicle;.
