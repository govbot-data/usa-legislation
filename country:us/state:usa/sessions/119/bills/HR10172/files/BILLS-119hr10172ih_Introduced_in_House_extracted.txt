R. 10172IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVESAugust 27, 2026Ms. Norton introduced the following bill; which was referred to the Committee on Veterans' AffairsA BILLTo amend title 38, United States Code, to extend eligibility for survivors' and dependents' educational assistance to the dependent parent of a person who dies of a service-connected disability or has a total and permanent service-connected disability, and for other purposes. 
1.Eligibility of a dependent parent for survivors' and dependents' educational assistance 
(a)EligibilitySection 3501(a)(1) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following new subparagraph:  (F)The dependent parent of a person described in subparagraph (A).. 
(b)Conforming amendments 
(1)Cessation of eligibilitySection 3511(b) of title 38, United States Code, is amended, in paragraphs (1), (2), and (4), by striking parent or spouse each place it appears and inserting parent, spouse, or child. (2)Periods of eligibilitySection 3512(b)(1)(A) of title 38, United States Code, is amended—
(A)by striking subparagraph (B) or (D) of section 3501(a)(1) of this title and inserting subparagraph (B), (D), or (F) of section 3501(a)(1) of this title; and (B)by striking or 3501(a)(1)(E) of this title and inserting 3501(a)(1)(E), or 3501(a)(1)(F) of this title. 
(3)Correspondence coursesSection 3534(b) of title 38, United States Code, is amended— (A)by striking eligible spouse or surviving spouse and inserting eligible spouse, surviving spouse, or eligible dependent parent;
(B)by striking such spouse’s entitlement and inserting such spouse’s or dependent parent’s entitlement; and (C)by striking paid to the spouse and inserting paid to the spouse or dependent parent.
