AN ACT 
To amend the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2023 to extend the time period for which certain regulations concerning the North Atlantic right whale are effective. 
 
 
1.Short titleThis Act may be cited as the Northeast Lobsterman Protection Act of 2026.  2.Extension of effectiveness of certain regulations concerning North Atlantic right whaleSection 101 of division JJ of the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2023 (16 U.S.C. 1387 note) is amended by striking 2028 each place it appears and inserting 2035. 
 Passed the House of Representatives September 1, 2026.Kevin F. McCumber,Clerk.
