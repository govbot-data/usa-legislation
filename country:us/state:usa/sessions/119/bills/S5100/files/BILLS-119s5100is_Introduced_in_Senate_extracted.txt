II119th CONGRESS2d SessionS. 5100IN THE SENATE OF THE UNITED STATESJuly 23, 2026Mr. Booker (for himself and Mr. Kim) introduced the following bill; which was read twice and referred to the Committee on Veterans’ AffairsA BILLTo name the community-based outpatient clinic of the Department of Veterans Affairs in Newton, New Jersey, as the Anthony Tony J. Gallopo VA Clinic.1.Name of Department of Veterans Affairs community-based outpatient clinic, Newton, New Jersey(a)In generalThe community-based outpatient clinic of the Department of Veterans Affairs in Newton, New Jersey, shall after the date of the enactment of this Act be known and designated as the Anthony Tony J. Gallopo Department of Veterans Affairs Clinic or the Anthony Tony J. Gallopo VA Clinic.(b)ReferencesAny reference in any law, regulation, map, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the clinic described in subsection (a) shall be considered to be a reference to the Anthony Tony J. Gallopo VA Clinic.
