R. 9888IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVESJuly 22, 2026Mr. Walkinshaw (for himself and Mr. Moylan) introduced the following bill; which was referred to the Committee on Armed ServicesA BILLTo direct the Secretary of Defense to ensure regular data collection on the costs and effects of extreme weather and incremental change at military installations, and for other purposes. 
1.Short title
 This Act may be cited as the Military Installation Readiness and Resilience Act of 2026. 
2.Data collection on cost and effects of extreme weather on Department of Defense military installationsThe Secretary of Defense shall ensure that the Under Secretary of Defense for Acquisition and Sustainment continues regular data collection on the costs and effects of extreme weather and incremental change at military installations and expand the scope of such data to include all types of natural disasters affecting military installations.
