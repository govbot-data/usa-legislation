R. 10816IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVESOctober 15, 2026Mrs. Dingell (for herself, Mr. Mfume, and Mr. Bell) introduced the following bill; which was referred to the Committee on Armed ServicesA BILLTo amend title 10, United States Code, to prohibit the naming of Department of Defense assets for sitting Presidents, and for other purposes. 
1.Short title
 This Act may be cited as Department of Defense Renaming Integrity and Ethics Act of 2026 or the DORIE Act of 2026.  
2.Prohibition on naming of Department of Defense assets for sitting Presidents 
(a)In generalChapter 2 of title 10, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following new section:  120a.Prohibition on naming of Department of Defense assets for sitting PresidentsNotwithstanding any other provision of law— 
(1)no facility, aircraft, vessel, or other asset of the Department of Defense may be named, renamed, designated, or redesignated in the name of a sitting President; and (2)none of the amounts authorized to be appropriated or otherwise made available for the Department of Defense for any fiscal year may be used to name, rename, designate, or redesignate any facility, aircraft, vessel, or other asset of the Department of Defense in the name of a sitting President..  
(b)ApplicabilityAny facility, aircraft, vessel, or other asset of the Department of Defense that, as of the date of the enactment of this Act, is named for the individual who is the President on such date shall be restored to the name of such facility, aircraft, vessel, or other asset immediately before the facility, aircraft, vessel, or other asset was named, renamed, designated, or redesignated in the name of a sitting President.
