R. 9871IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVESJuly 22, 2026Ms. Plaskett introduced the following bill; which was referred to the Committee on Veterans' AffairsA BILLTo name the Department of Veterans Affairs community-based outpatient clinic, Saint Thomas, United States Virgin Islands, as the Sergeant First Class Floyd E. Lake Department of Veterans Affairs Community-Based Outpatient Clinic. 
1.Name of Department of Veterans Affairs community-based outpatient clinic, Saint Thomas, United States Virgin IslandsThe Department of Veterans Affairs community-based outpatient clinic in Saint Thomas, United States Virgin Islands, shall after the date of the enactment of this Act be known and designated as the Sergeant First Class Floyd E. Lake Department of Veterans Affairs Community-Based Outpatient Clinic. Any reference to such clinic in any law, regulation, map, document, record, or other paper of the United States shall be considered to be a reference to the Sergeant First Class Floyd E. Lake Department of Veterans Affairs Community-Based Outpatient Clinic.
