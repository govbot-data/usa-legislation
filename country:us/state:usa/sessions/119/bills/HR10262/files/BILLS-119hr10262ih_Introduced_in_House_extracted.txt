R. 10262IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVESSeptember 3, 2026Mrs. Fletcher (for herself and Mr. Pfluger) introduced the following bill; which was referred to the Committee on Energy and CommerceA BILLTo require the Secretary of Energy to request the National Petroleum Council to issue a report with respect to the Strategic Petroleum Reserve, and for other purposes. 
1.Short titleThis Act may be cited as the 21st Century Strategic Petroleum Reserve Act or the 21st Century SPR Act. 2.Report on Strategic Petroleum Reserve modernization (a)In generalNot later than 90 days after the date of enactment of this section, the Secretary of Energy shall request that the National Petroleum Council— 
(1)not later than one year after so requested, submit to the Secretary of Energy and Congress a report containing— (A)an analysis of opportunities to improve and modernize the physical infrastructure and operation of the Strategic Petroleum Reserve to respond to petroleum product supply shocks, including by— 
(i)expanding the geographic distribution of the Strategic Petroleum Reserve;  (ii)expanding the Strategic Petroleum Reserve to include storage of refined petroleum products; 
(iii)eliminating congressionally mandated sales of petroleum products from the Strategic Petroleum Reserve; (iv)creating storage facilities sufficiently resilient to withstand drawdowns of petroleum products of the volume and frequency required over the last 10 years of the Strategic Petroleum Reserve’s use; 
(v)expanding the storage capacity of the Strategic Petroleum Reserve;  (vi)encouraging purchases of petroleum products to replenish the Strategic Petroleum Reserve during periods of low petroleum prices; and 
(vii)increasing— (I)the Strategic Petroleum Reserve’s rate of fill; and 
(II)the bidirectional flow capability of the Strategic Petroleum Reserve’s pipeline infrastructure; (B)any recommendations for Federal agencies and Congress to improve and modernize the physical infrastructure and operation of the Strategic Petroleum Reserve to respond to petroleum product supply shocks; and 
(C)an analysis and evaluation of the Life Extension II project, including work that was originally in-scope for the Life Extension II project but was subsequently not carried out; and  (2)make publicly available the report submitted under paragraph (1). 
(b)DefinitionsIn this section: (1)Petroleum productThe term petroleum product has the meaning given such term in section 3 of the Energy Policy and Conservation Act (42 U.S.C. 6202). 
(2)Storage facilityThe term storage facility has the meaning given such term in section 152 of the Energy Policy and Conservation Act (42 U.S.C. 6232). (3)Strategic Petroleum ReserveThe term Strategic Petroleum Reserve has the meaning given such term in section 152 of the Energy Policy and Conservation Act (42 U.S.C. 6232).
