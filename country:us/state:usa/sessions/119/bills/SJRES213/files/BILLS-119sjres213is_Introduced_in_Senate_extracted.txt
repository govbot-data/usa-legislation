RES. 213IN THE SENATE OF THE UNITED STATESSeptember 14, 2026Mr. Durbin (for himself, Mr. Blumenthal, Mr. Padilla, Mr. Whitehouse, Mrs. Shaheen, Mr. Markey, Mr. Van Hollen, Mr. Wyden, Mr. Welch, Mr. King, Mr. Kaine, and Mr. Booker) introduced the following joint resolution; which was read twice and referred to the Committee on the JudiciaryJOINT RESOLUTIONProviding for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement of the Department of Homeland Security relating to Establishing a Fixed Time Period of Admission and an Extension of Stay Procedure for Nonimmigrant Academic Students, Exchange Visitors, and Representatives of Foreign Information Media.That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement of the Department of Homeland Security relating to Establishing a Fixed Time Period of Admission and an Extension of Stay Procedure for Nonimmigrant Academic Students, Exchange Visitors, and Representatives of Foreign Information Media (91 Fed. Reg. 44976 (July 17, 2026)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
