RES. 213IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVESAugust 10, 2026Mr. Gallagher (for himself and Mr. Fong) submitted the following joint resolution; which was referred to the Committee on Energy and CommerceJOINT RESOLUTIONProviding for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule issued by the Environmental Protection Agency relating to the California State Nonroad Engine Pollution Control Standards; Commercial Harbor Craft Regulations; Notice of Decision. 
That Congress disapproves the rule issued by the Environmental Protection Agency relating to the California State Nonroad Engine Pollution Control Standards; Commercial Harbor Craft Regulations; Notice of Decision (90 Fed. Reg. 1998 (January 10, 2025)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
