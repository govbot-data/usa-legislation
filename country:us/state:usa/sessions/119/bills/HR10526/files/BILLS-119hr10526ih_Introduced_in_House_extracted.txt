Reynolds Post Office Building. 
1.Officer Blake A. Reynolds Post Office Building 
(a)DesignationThe facility of the United States Postal Service located at 501 West Memorial Drive in Muncie, Indiana, shall be known and designated as the Officer Blake A. Reynolds Post Office Building. (b)ReferencesAny reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the Officer Blake A. Reynolds Post Office Building.
