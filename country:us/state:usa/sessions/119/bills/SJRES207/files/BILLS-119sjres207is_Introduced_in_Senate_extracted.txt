            relating to California State Motor Vehicle Pollution Control Standards; Notice of

                Clean Car Program and a Within the Scope Conformation for California's Zero Emission
 Vehicle Amendments for 2017 and Earlier Model Years.That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Environmental Protection Agency relating to California State Motor Vehicle Pollution Control Standards; Notice of Decision Granting a Waiver of Clean Air Act Preemption for California's Advanced Clean Car Program and a Within the Scope Conformation for California's Zero Emission Vehicle Amendments for 2017 and Earlier Model Years (78 Fed.
